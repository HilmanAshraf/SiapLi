Privacy notice

SiapLI is built so your resume stays yours. This note explains, in plain terms, what happens to your data.

What we read

When you upload a resume, we read it once to extract the text. Before anything else happens, we remove personal details — your name, email, phone number, ID number, and links.

What we keep

We keep only the anonymised list of skills needed to match you to internships. We do not store your raw file, and we do not keep contact details. You can delete your results at any time from the results page.

What we never do

We do not sell data, we do not track you across the web, and we do not require an account. Match scores are guidance to help you decide — they are never used to rank or filter you without a human in the loop.

For universities

The cohort dashboard shows only aggregated, anonymous figures. Groups smaller than five students are hidden so no individual can be identified.

Questions

This is a student project built for a hackathon. The data shown in the app is sample data for demonstration.
