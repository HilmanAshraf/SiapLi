- **Top-3 agreement: 100.0%** (25/25 resumes). The intended role appeared in SiapLI's top-3 matches.

## Honest limitations

- Ground truth is a proxy (intended role), not independent lecturer judgement — the real study should use the industrial training unit's rankings.
- Sample size is small (n=25). Results indicate direction, not statistical significance.
- The skills dictionary is not exhaustive, so a niche skill could be missed in extraction.
