## Our 4 differentiators (speaker notes)
1. **Readiness, not just listings** — we answer "am I ready, what next", not just "which job".
2. **University cohort dashboard** — no job board shows a career unit aggregate cohort skill gaps.
3. **Internship-specific, Malaysia-first** — tuned to LI roles and the Year 2–3 timeline.
4. **Explainable** — we show which skills matched/missed, not a black-box score.

## Market context (one-liners)
- MY employers report persistent graduate digital-skills gaps.
- Fresh grads increasingly struggle as AI automates entry-level roles.
- Talent gap is "credentials, not job-ready skills" — exactly our target.
